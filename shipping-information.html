Real-World Details

What most brands don't tell you.

Shipping isn't always smooth. Here's how we handle the situations nobody else talks about.

Ramadan & Eid Season

Courier volumes surge up to 3x during the last 10 days of Ramadan and the week before Eid. Add 1–2 extra days to your expected delivery. Ordering early is always a good idea.

Monsoon & Rainy Season

Heavy monsoon can delay deliveries in Karachi and coastal areas by 1–2 days. Our moisture-resistant packaging keeps your garments safe even if the parcel sits in a wet courier van.

Sale & Peak Weeks

During major sales and festive drops, our studio packs hundreds of parcels a day. Dispatches stay on time but courier pickup may slip by a few hours. Your tracking number will confirm when it's on the way.

Public Holidays & Sundays

Couriers don't operate on Sundays and national holidays. Orders confirmed on a Sunday or holiday dispatch the next working day. This is factored into your delivery estimate already.

If You Miss the Delivery

The courier attempts up to 3 times on consecutive days and calls you before arriving. If all attempts fail, the parcel returns to us. We can reship it for a Rs. 200 redelivery fee.

Gated Societies & Hostels

If you live in a gated community, hostel or apartment building, courier often delivers to the main gate or reception. Please share a nearby landmark and the exact block/floor so nothing goes to the wrong person.

Pro Tips

Write your address the right way.

90% of delivery delays we see are caused by unclear addresses. Here's how to avoid them completely.

Do This

Include a Nearby Landmark

"House 21, Street 4, Block C, near Ideal Bakery, DHA Phase 5, Lahore" — courier riders navigate by landmarks, not just street numbers. This single detail speeds up delivery dramatically.

Avoid This

"Karachi, near my cousin's house"

Vague addresses like this cause deliveries to fail. Without a street name, block number, or landmark, the courier has no way to find you and the parcel returns to us.

Do This

Give a WhatsApp-Active Number

The number you share must be reachable on WhatsApp or by call this is how the courier contacts you before arriving. A number that's switched off is the #1 cause of failed deliveries.

Avoid This

Multiple Addresses in One Message

Don't send two or three addresses and say "any of these work". It confuses our packing team and delays dispatch. Pick one clear address and stick with it.

Shipping FAQs

Your shipping questions.

Answers to the most common questions our team gets asked on WhatsApp about delivery.

Can I change my delivery address after placing the order?

Yes — but only before dispatch. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and the new address. Once the parcel is handed to the courier, the address is locked and cannot be changed.

How do I check the status of my delivery?

Two ways: use the tracking number we send you on WhatsApp and enter it on the courier's website, or message us directly with your name and order date. We reply with a status update within minutes during business hours.

Do you offer express or next-day delivery?

We currently offer standard delivery only. Express options may be added in the future, but for now, 2–4 working days is our fastest guaranteed timeline. Messages about "urgent" orders can't override courier schedules.

Is Cash on Delivery available for all cities?

Yes — COD is available on every delivery address in Pakistan, including remote areas. There is no prepayment required. You inspect the parcel and pay the courier in cash at the time of delivery.

Can someone else receive my parcel?

Yes. As long as the person at your address knows an order is arriving and can pay the COD amount, the courier will hand it over. Please mention this to the person who'll be home, and share the expected total with them.

What happens if my parcel is lost in transit?

This is extremely rare, but if it happens, we handle everything. Contact us on WhatsApp we open a formal claim with the courier, and once confirmed lost (usually 5–7 days), we reship your order free of charge. You never bear the loss.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Not at the moment. Softgarments currently delivers only within Pakistan. International shipping is something we'd love to offer in the future — if you're outside Pakistan and interested, WhatsApp us and we'll add you to our waitlist.

Why is my tracking number not updating?

Courier systems update at set checkpoints not every hour. If your tracking shows no movement for more than 48 hours, message us with your number. Sometimes the parcel has moved but the scan hasn't been logged yet.

Can I ask the courier to leave the parcel at my door?

No — because of Cash on Delivery, the courier must collect payment in person. You cannot ask them to "leave it and take the money later". If you won't be home, please arrange for someone else to receive it.

Why did my delivery estimate change after ordering?

Occasionally, once a parcel reaches the courier's sorting facility, they may find your area requires a longer route than expected. In rare cases, weather, strikes, or road closures on the route can extend timelines. If this happens, we'll message you proactively never let you wonder.
