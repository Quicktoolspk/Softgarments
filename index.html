Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with SoftGarments — from MOQs to shipping and sampling.

Our MOQ varies by product category. For basic styles, we offer MOQs as low as 50–100 pieces per design. For custom development with unique fabrics or trims, MOQs may be higher. We work with startups and small brands to find flexible solutions.

Lead times depend on order complexity. Sampling takes 2–4 weeks, and bulk production typically takes 4–8 weeks from sample approval. We offer expedited options for urgent orders.

Yes — sampling is an essential part of our process. We provide accurate physical samples for fit, fabric, and construction review before moving to bulk. We can also do multiple rounds of sampling if needed.

Absolutely. We offer full private label services including custom neck labels, hang tags, care labels, poly bags, and premium packaging. You can provide your own materials or we can source them for you.

Yes — we ship worldwide via air and sea freight. We handle all logistics including customs clearance, documentation, and door-to-door delivery. We ship to over 40 countries with reliable tracking.

We offer flexible payment terms including 50% deposit and 50% balance before shipment, or 30/70 for larger orders. We accept bank transfers, LC, and other arrangements based on mutual agreement.
