Search Tips

Do this, not that.

Small changes in how you type make a big difference in what we can find for you.

Do this

Be specific, but simple

Try: "linen co-ord set in beige" — colour + fabric + product type works best.

Mention the occasion

Try: "something for a mehndi in Lahore" — we'll send options that fit the dress code.

Share your size upfront

Try: "medium kurti, budget Rs. 2,500" — instant narrowing, faster reply.

Use Urdu if you like

Try: "mujhe white kurti chahiye summer ke liye" — we understand both.

Reference a past order

Try: "same style as my Lira Kurti but in blue" — we'll pull up your history.

Avoid this

Too vague

Avoid: "koi achi si dress dikhao" — we don't know where to start. Give us one anchor.

Product codes that don't exist

Avoid: "SG-1044" unless it's from an old order. We don't use code-based searches.

Multiple requests in one line

Avoid: "kurti + shirt + maybe some trousers" — one search, one answer.

"Best one" without detail

Avoid: "which is best?" — what's best for you depends on your need. Tell us your need.

Screenshots from other brands

Avoid sending competitor photos. Send inspiration links — we'll suggest something similar.

Search FAQs

Your search questions.

Common questions about how our search and product discovery works.

Can I search in Urdu?

Yes — and we encourage it. You can type "mujhe summer ke liye white kurti chahiye" and our team will understand exactly what you mean. Urdu, Roman Urdu and English all work on WhatsApp.

Do I need to know product names or SKU codes?

No. Our team works with product descriptions, not codes. Describe what you're looking for in your own words — we'll match it to our catalogue.

How often are trending searches updated?

The trending tags on this page update weekly based on real WhatsApp conversations and website activity. The Top 10 table refreshes every Monday.

Why don't you have a live search box on the website?

Because our entire catalogue (200+ products with variants) lives on WhatsApp where a real person can help you. A static search box would miss half the ways customers ask for things. If you type "I want something nice for a wedding", no algorithm beats a human who knows the collection.

Can I search by colour?

Yes. Colour-first searches work well: "beige co-ord", "white kurti", "black formal shirt". We'll send photos of everything matching that shade.

Can I search by budget?

Absolutely. Mention your budget upfront — "kurti under Rs. 2,500" — and we'll only send options that fit. It saves time for both of us.

Do you keep a record of my past searches?

Your WhatsApp conversations stay in your chat history and our order log — helpful when you want "the same as last time". We don't build behavioural profiles or track you across other sites.

What if I only remember the colour and vibe, not the product?

That's totally fine — it's the most common type of search we get. Say something like "something soft and pastel for a daytime event" and we'll curate 4–5 matching pieces for you.
