Softgarments ("we", "our", "us") operates softgarments.pk and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy & Cookie Policy describes how we collect, use and share your data when you browse our website, place an order via WhatsApp, or interact with our brand. By using our services, you agree to this policy.

01 Introduction

Softgarments is a Pakistani online clothing brand offering premium women's clothing — including kurtis, tops, co-ord sets, and occasion wear — delivered nationwide with Cash on Delivery. Because we primarily take orders through WhatsApp, most of the personal information we receive is what you voluntarily share with us during an order conversation.

This policy applies to all visitors and customers, regardless of where you access our website from within Pakistan.

02 Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to process your order and improve your experience. This includes:

Information you provide directly

  • Order details: name, phone number, delivery address, city, and postal code.
  • Communication: messages you send via WhatsApp, email or social media.
  • Sizing preferences: measurements you share for a better fit.
  • Feedback: reviews, ratings and photos you voluntarily share.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: browser type, operating system, screen size.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, time spent, referring source.
  • IP address: used for analytics and basic security.

We never collect your CNIC, bank account number, credit card details or any sensitive financial information. Our Cash on Delivery model means you pay only in person at the time of delivery.

03 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Order fulfilment: processing your order, dispatching it, and confirming delivery.
  • Customer support: answering sizing questions, tracking orders, and arranging exchanges.
  • Service updates: notifying you about order status via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Improvements: understanding which products and styles are most loved, so we can improve our collection.
  • Legal compliance: meeting our obligations under Pakistani law.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or targeted advertising on third-party platforms without your consent.

04 Sharing Your Data

We share your information only with trusted parties who help us serve you, and only to the extent necessary:

Recipient Purpose Data Shared
Courier partners Delivering your order across Pakistan Name, phone, address, order details
WhatsApp (Meta) Order communication and customer support Messages, phone number

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

05 Cookies & Tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. Softgarments uses a limited set of cookies to keep the site functioning smoothly and to understand how visitors use it.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to work (page navigation, security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference cookies: remember settings such as language, so you don't have to select them again.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand which pages are visited most (anonymised data).
  • Third-party cookies: may be set by services we embed (e.g., Instagram Reels, Google Fonts).

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block all cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies from being set.

Please note: disabling essential cookies may affect how the website functions.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. These include:

  • Secure HTTPS connection across our website.
  • Restricted access to customer order data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular review of our communication and storage practices.

While we do our best, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you ever suspect that your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately on WhatsApp.

07 Your Rights

As a customer of Softgarments, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate details we have on file.
  • Request deletion of your data (except where we are legally required to retain it).
  • Opt out of promotional messages at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential cookies.

To exercise any of these rights, simply message us on WhatsApp at +92 337 9022920 or email softgarmentspk@gmail.com. We will respond within 7 working days.

08 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order records: retained for up to 3 years for accounting and warranty purposes.
  • Customer conversations: kept for up to 2 years to help with exchanges and follow-ups.
  • Analytics data: stored in anonymised form, typically up to 26 months.

After the retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

09 Children's Privacy

Softgarments sells women's clothing designed for adults. Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

We may update this Privacy & Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of our website or services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
