Withdrawal Policy

Your right to change your mind.

Sometimes plans change. Sometimes you order two sizes and want to cancel one. Sometimes you simply change your mind. This page explains your right to withdraw from a purchase — clearly, honestly, and without the fine print.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

The short version

You can cancel your order anytime before dispatch, free of charge. After dispatch, you can still refuse delivery at your doorstep — you simply don't pay, and we take the parcel back. Because we operate on Cash on Delivery only, no money ever leaves your hands until you've inspected the parcel. That's the whole policy in 2 sentences.

Understand the Difference

Withdrawal vs Return.

These two terms sound similar — but they describe two very different moments in your shopping journey. Here's how they differ.

Withdrawal

Cancel before or at delivery

Withdrawal is about backing out of a purchase before you accept the parcel. No product is exchanged, no money changes hands. You simply say "I don't want this anymore."

  • Before dispatch: Message us — order is cancelled, nothing owed.
  • During transit: Refuse delivery — parcel returns to us.
  • At doorstep: Inspect and politely decline — you pay nothing.
  • No reason needed: You don't have to explain why.
  • Zero cost: No withdrawal fee, no shipping charge, no hassle.
Return / Exchange

After you've accepted the parcel

A return or exchange is about what happens after you've paid and accepted the product. You already own it — now you want to swap it or fix an issue.

  • Wrong size: Exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • Defect or damage: Free replacement, reported within 7 days.
  • Wrong item: Full exchange at our cost.
  • Rs. 200 return fee applies for size exchanges.
  • Condition matters: Original tags, unworn, unwashed.

Exceptions

When withdrawal isn't possible.

We keep these exceptions minimal — but honesty means mentioning them upfront.

Custom-stitched pieces

Items made to your specific measurements cannot be withdrawn once production has started. Withdrawal window closes when stitching begins.

Final sale items

Products marked "Final Sale" or purchased at clearance above 50% off cannot be withdrawn or exchanged after payment.

Personal care products

Innerwear, shapewear and intimate apparel cannot be withdrawn after acceptance due to hygiene reasons — but you can still refuse delivery.

Repeat cancellations

We track repeat cancellations. If a customer withdraws more than 3 consecutive orders without reason, future orders may require WhatsApp verification first.

Refused without inspection

You may open and inspect the parcel before paying. Refusing without opening is allowed, but you waive the right to report defect or wrong-item on that parcel.

Always allowed

Withdrawal before dispatch is always allowed — no exceptions, no penalties, no questions asked. This is your unqualified right.

Your rights under Pakistani consumer law

Pakistan's Consumer Protection Act grants consumers the right to be protected against unfair trade practices, the right to information, and the right to seek redressal. Softgarments' withdrawal policy exceeds the minimum requirements of this law — we offer free pre-dispatch cancellation even where the law doesn't strictly require it.

If you believe your consumer rights have been violated in any interaction with Softgarments, you can escalate directly to our team on WhatsApp at +92 337 9022920 and we will address the matter within 3 working days.

Withdrawal FAQs

Your withdrawal questions.

Answers to the questions customers ask most often about cancelling an order.

Is there a time limit to withdraw an order?

Yes — but it's generous. You can withdraw anytime before the courier hands you the parcel. Once you accept and pay, the withdrawal window closes and the returns policy applies instead. For most orders, that gives you a window of 2–4 working days.

Do I need a reason to cancel?

Never. We don't require an explanation. If you want to tell us why, you're welcome to — it helps us improve. But "I changed my mind" is a complete and valid reason for us.

Will I be charged any fee for cancelling?

No. Withdrawal before dispatch is 100% free. Once dispatched, if you refuse the parcel at your doorstep, you still pay nothing — because with Cash on Delivery, you simply don't hand over any money. There is no cancellation fee at any stage.

Can I withdraw only part of my order?

Yes. You can cancel one item while keeping another — just message us with the specific product you want removed. We'll recalculate the total and confirm on WhatsApp. Note that if the remaining value drops below Rs. 3,000, the Rs. 200 delivery charge will re-apply.

I already paid — can I still withdraw?

Once you've paid and accepted the parcel, withdrawal is no longer applicable — but the Return & Exchange policy applies instead. If you haven't yet paid but the courier has arrived, you can still refuse delivery freely.

What if the courier doesn't accept my refusal?

Couriers in Pakistan are contractually bound to accept refusals. If a rider pressures you or refuses to take the parcel back, note their name, vehicle number and time — then message us immediately on WhatsApp. We'll resolve it directly with the courier company and ensure you're not charged.

Can I withdraw and immediately place a new order?

Absolutely — this is common. Customers often withdraw one size and reorder another. Message us with both requests and we'll handle it in one conversation. No penalty, no delay in your new order.

Do repeated cancellations affect my account?

We understand life gets busy. But if we see a pattern of 3+ consecutive cancellations without reason, we may ask for WhatsApp verification on future orders before dispatch. This protects our team's time and keeps prices low for all customers.

What happens to my order if I don't respond to the courier?

The courier will attempt delivery up to 3 times over consecutive days. If all attempts fail — because you weren't reachable or declined to respond — the parcel returns to us and the order is automatically cancelled at no cost to you. You can reorder anytime.

How do I know my withdrawal was received?

You'll get a WhatsApp confirmation from us within minutes during business hours (10 AM – 8 PM, Mon-Sat). If you don't hear back within 2 hours, message us again — sometimes messages get buried in busy periods, but we never ignore them.

Can I withdraw a gift order?

Yes. The person who placed the order is the one who must withdraw it — so ask the gift-giver to message us. If they'd prefer, we can also arrange for the parcel to be delivered to a different recipient instead.

What if I mistakenly confirm the order and then realize my size is wrong?

Perfect timing — just message us immediately. As long as the parcel hasn't been dispatched, we can change the size (or the product entirely) at no cost. Once dispatched, you can still withdraw and reorder — or exchange after delivery.

Want to cancel right now?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order details — we'll confirm the cancellation in minutes. No forms, no waiting, no fine print.
