Eligibility

What we accept — and what we don't.

Honest rules, no fine print. Read both columns before messaging us — it saves time for both sides.

We Accept

Eligible for return / exchange
  • Wrong size: Exchange within 7 days. Rs. 200 return fee applies.
  • Wrong item delivered: Full replacement at our cost.
  • Manufacturing defect: Stitching, buttons, zips — free replacement.
  • Damaged in transit: Report with photos immediately — we handle it.
  • Significant colour mismatch: Exchange if shade differs notably from listing.
  • Fabric quality issues: After washing, if fabric fails — replacement.

We Don't Accept

Not eligible for return
  • Change of mind: No refunds after payment — exchange only.
  • Used or washed items: Must be in original condition.
  • Tags removed: Original tags and packaging must be intact.
  • Custom-stitched items: Made-to-order pieces are non-returnable.
  • Returns after 7 days: Requests after the window aren't entertained.
  • Final sale items: Clearance pieces above 50% off are non-exchangeable.

Know the Difference

Return vs Exchange.

These two words get mixed up all the time. Here's how they actually differ at Softgarments.

Return Sending it back

You've received the item and want a refund. Only applies when the mistake is on our side — defect, wrong item, or damage.

When: Defect, wrong item, or damage in transit.
Refund: Bank transfer, EasyPaisa, or store credit.
Timeline: 5–7 working days after inspection.
Shipping: Fully covered by Softgarments.
VS

Exchange Swapping it

The item is fine, but the size or colour isn't right. Swap it for the correct variant without any cash refund.

When: Size issue, colour preference, or choosing different product.
Refund: No cash — store credit if the item is out of stock.
Timeline: Replacement dispatched within 3–5 days.
Shipping: Rs. 200 return fee unless it's our fault.

Pack it Right

Return FAQs

Your return questions.

Straight answers to the questions we get most often about returns and exchanges.

How long do I have to return or exchange an item?

You have 7 days from the date of delivery to raise a return or exchange request. Requests raised after 7 days cannot be entertained, regardless of the reason.

Do I have to pay for return shipping?

If the issue is on our end — wrong item, defect, or damage — we cover the return shipping. For customer-initiated size or colour exchanges, a Rs. 200 return fee applies.

Can I return an item if I simply changed my mind?

We do not offer cash refunds for change of mind after payment. But you can exchange the item for a different size or product of equal value within 7 days. If the new item is cheaper, the difference is issued as store credit.

How do I raise a return request?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at +92 337 9022920 with your order details and the reason for return. Attach 1–2 clear photos of the item. We respond within 24 hours.

What if the item I want to exchange for is out of stock?

If your preferred size or colour is unavailable, we'll offer you store credit equal to the item's value — valid for 6 months. Alternatively, you can choose a different product of equal or higher value (you pay the difference).

Can I exchange a product I bought on sale?

Yes, if it's from a standard sale (up to 50% off), you can exchange for size or colour within 7 days. Items marked "Final Sale" or discounted above 50% cannot be exchanged or returned.

What if I receive a defective or damaged item?

Take photos immediately (before wearing or washing) and message us within 24 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a free replacement with no return shipping cost. In rare cases where the same item isn't available, we'll process a full refund.

How long does the entire return take?

Total timeline is typically 4–6 working days: 24 hours for approval, 1–2 days for pickup/shipping back to us, 1–2 days for inspection, and 1–2 days for dispatching the replacement or processing the refund.

Do you offer cash refunds?

Cash refunds are processed only in cases where we cannot provide a replacement (e.g., the item is permanently out of stock). In all other cases, exchanges and store credits are the standard resolution.

What if I've already washed or worn the item?

Unfortunately, we can't accept returns on items that have been washed, worn outside, or had tags removed. This is standard for hygiene and resale reasons. If the fabric has a genuine defect that only became visible after washing, message us with photos — we'll investigate case by case.

Can I exchange a gift order?

Yes — the person who placed the order can initiate the exchange. Alternatively, they can message us authorising you to handle it directly, and we'll process it with your contact number.

Who pays for the courier if the return is our fault?

We do. If the mistake was ours — wrong item, defect, damage — we'll arrange pickup at no cost to you. You won't pay a single rupee for the return shipping or the replacement.
