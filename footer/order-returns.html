Returns & Exchange

Easy returns, honest policy.

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn't right, here's what we can and can't do.

What we accept

  • Wrong size: Exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • Wrong item delivered: Full replacement at no extra cost.
  • Manufacturing defect: Free replacement if reported within 7 days.
  • Damaged in transit: Report immediately with photos — we'll sort it.
  • Colour mismatch: Exchange if the shade is significantly different from the listing.

What we can't accept

  • Change of mind: We don't offer refunds for a change of preference.
  • Used or washed items: All exchanges must be in original condition.
  • Tags removed: Items must retain original tags and packaging.
  • Custom-stitched items: Made-to-order pieces are non-exchangeable.
  • Returns after 7 days: Requests raised after 7 days won't be entertained.

FAQs

Common questions.

Everything you might be wondering about ordering and returning — answered.

Can I pay online or by bank transfer?

Currently we offer Cash on Delivery only. This lets you inspect your order before paying, so you always know exactly what you're receiving. We may add online payments in the future.

Do you deliver to my city?

Yes — we deliver to every city and town in Pakistan. If your area has courier coverage, we can reach you. Message us your city name on WhatsApp if you're unsure.

What if the courier tries to deliver but I'm not home?

The courier will attempt delivery up to 3 times over consecutive days. They'll also call the phone number you provided. If delivery fails entirely, the package returns to us — and we can reschedule at a delivery charge of Rs. 200.

How do I know my size before ordering?

Use our Size Guide on the home page, or simply share your measurements (bust, waist, hip) with us on WhatsApp. Our team will recommend the best size for you before you order.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes — you can cancel anytime before dispatch. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details. Once dispatched, orders cannot be cancelled but can be exchanged after delivery.

What if the fabric or colour looks different from the photos?

We do our best to photograph every product in natural light with no colour filters. Slight variation may occur due to screen settings, but if you feel the item is significantly different from the listing, contact us within 7 days — we'll arrange an exchange.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer exchanges and replacements, not cash refunds. If your item cannot be replaced (e.g., sold out), we'll issue store credit valid for 6 months on your next order.

Who pays for the return shipping?

If the issue is on our end (wrong item, defect, damage), we cover the return shipping. For size exchanges initiated by the customer, a Rs. 200 return fee applies.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, we'll send you a tracking number and courier name on WhatsApp. You can also message us anytime for a status update — we reply within minutes during business hours.

Is there a minimum order value?

No minimum order. However, orders above Rs. 3,000 qualify for free delivery. Below that, a flat Rs. 200 delivery charge applies anywhere in Pakistan.

Still have questions?

Our team is available on WhatsApp every day. Ask about sizing, delivery, exchanges — anything at all. We reply fast.
