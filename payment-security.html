Scam Awareness

Real scam vs real Softgarments.

Fake COD scams are on the rise in Pakistan. Here's how to tell a genuine message from a fraudster using our name.

Red Flag — It's a Scam

What a scam message looks like

Fake callers claim to be from "Softgarments" and demand money to release your parcel. Here's what they typically say — and why each one is a lie.

"Hello, this is Softgarments. Your parcel is stuck at our delivery hub. Please send Rs. 800 via EasyPaisa to release it."
"Aap ka parcel ruk gaya hai. Advance payment karein, phir deliver karenge."
"Please share the 6-digit code we sent you to confirm delivery."

All of these are lies. We never ask for money to "release" a parcel, never take advance, and never ask for OTPs.

Green Flag — It's Us

What a genuine message looks like

Every real Softgarments message comes from our verified WhatsApp number and follows a consistent, transparent format.

"Salaam! Your order for Lira Floral Kurti (Size M) is confirmed. Total: Rs. 1,650 with delivery. Arriving in 2–4 working days. — Team Softgarments"
"Your parcel has been dispatched. Tracking number: LP-XXXX. You'll pay Rs. 1,650 to the courier on delivery."
"The courier will call you before arriving. Please keep the exact amount ready. — Team Softgarments"

Real messages always: come from our saved number, mention your exact product, and never ask for advance payment.

Courier Verification

Before you pay, verify.

We've built in 5 verification checkpoints. Use them every time — even if you trust the brand.

Check the Tamper Seal

Your parcel should have our branded tamper-evident seal intact. If it's broken, torn, or missing — do not accept the parcel.

2

Confirm the Tracking Number

The courier's printed slip must match the tracking number we sent you on WhatsApp. Mismatches indicate a wrong or fake parcel.

3

Open and Inspect First

You have the right to open the parcel before paying. Check the product, size, colour and quality. If it's wrong, refuse the parcel and contact us.

4

Match the Amount Exactly

The courier's demanded amount must equal the total we quoted on WhatsApp — nothing extra, ever. If they ask for more, refuse and message us instantly.

5

Get a Receipt

Ask the courier for a printed receipt or slip. If they can't provide one, note their name and vehicle number before paying.

Your Rights

What you're entitled to — always.

These aren't favours. These are your basic rights as a Softgarments customer.

Right to Inspect

You may open, examine, and even try on the item before paying the courier. Nothing is owed until you're satisfied it matches the order.

Right to Refuse

If the product, size, or colour doesn't match what you ordered, you can refuse delivery entirely. No questions, no penalties, no pressure.

Right to Exact Price

You never pay a rupee more than the amount we confirmed on WhatsApp. Any deviation is a courier issue — report it immediately.

Right to Refund If Lost

If your parcel is lost in transit (rare, but possible), we reship your order free of charge. You bear no financial loss.

Right to Real Support

You can message us on WhatsApp at any time — before, during, or after delivery. A real person replies. No bots, no automated loops.

Right to Privacy

Your phone number, address and order details are used only for delivering your parcel. We never sell, share, or spam.

Our Security Practices

What we do to keep you safe.

Trust is earned, not claimed. Here's the behind-the-scenes security work you'll never see — but always benefit from.

Encrypted WhatsApp Communication

Every order conversation happens on WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted platform. Nobody — not even WhatsApp — can read your address or order details in transit.

Manual Order Verification

Before dispatch, our team manually reviews every order — cross-checking address, phone number, and item. Automated systems can miss fraud patterns; humans don't.

Trusted Courier Networks Only

We ship exclusively through Leopards, TCS, and M&P — established, trackable, accountable. We never use informal couriers or unverified riders.

Limited Data Retention

We keep your order details only as long as necessary — up to 3 years for accounting, then securely deleted. We never build marketing profiles.

Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your phone number, name and address are shared with the courier only for delivery. We don't sell, rent, or trade data with anyone else. Ever.

Public Scam Alerts

When we detect new scam attempts using our name, we post warnings on our Instagram to alert all customers within minutes. Transparency over silence.

Payment FAQs

Your payment questions.

Real answers to the questions we get most often about payment and security.

Do you take advance payment from any customer?

No. Zero exceptions. Softgarments is Cash on Delivery only. If someone claims we do require advance payment, they are not us — please report that number to us immediately on WhatsApp.

Can I pay via EasyPaisa or JazzCash?

Not currently. Our entire payment process is built around Cash on Delivery — simpler, safer, and it lets you inspect the item before paying. Mobile wallet support may be added in the future, but never as a mandatory option.

What if the courier demands more than the quoted amount?

This should never happen. If it does — refuse to pay the extra, take the courier's name and vehicle number, and message us immediately. We'll resolve it with the courier and prevent it from happening again.

I got a call asking for advance payment. What should I do?

It's a scam. Do not send money. Note the caller's number, block them, and report to us on WhatsApp. We'll add that number to our scam watchlist and post a public warning if we see a pattern.

How do I know it's really you on WhatsApp?

Our only official WhatsApp number is +92 337 9022920. Save it in your contacts. Real messages from us always come from this number — never from a random 0300 or 0345 number claiming to be us.

Is my phone number safe with you?

Yes. Your phone number is used only for order communication and courier coordination. We never share it with third-party marketing, advertisers, or data brokers — ever.

Can I get a receipt after paying cash?

Yes. The courier should provide a printed slip on payment. If they don't, ask them for it — or message us and we'll send you a digital receipt on WhatsApp within 24 hours.

What happens if I pay and then find an issue with the item?

That's why we recommend inspecting before paying. But if you miss something, message us within 24 hours with photos. We'll arrange an exchange according to our Returns Policy.

Can I ask the courier to leave the parcel without payment?

No. Because the payment is Cash on Delivery, the courier must collect the amount in person before handing over the parcel. This is standard for all legitimate COD deliveries in Pakistan.

Do you offer refunds if I change my mind?

We offer exchanges and replacements, not cash refunds. However, if you refuse delivery entirely (before paying), there's nothing to refund — you simply haven't paid yet. This is one of the biggest safety benefits of COD.

Suspicious about something? Ask us first.

If you ever receive a message, call, or parcel that doesn't feel right — just message us directly. We reply in minutes and we'll tell you the truth.
