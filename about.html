The Beginning

Born from a simple frustration.

We couldn't find everyday women's clothing in Pakistan that was comfortable, breathable, and actually well-made the kind you'd reach for again and again. Too often, softness meant compromise, and quality meant an unreasonable price tag.

So we started making it ourselves. Every Softgarments piece from premium cotton kurtis to linen co-ord sets and festive wear begins with fabric selection. We test softness, breathability and how it holds up after 30+ washes, before it ever becomes a product. Then comes the fit: real bodies, real sizing, no guesswork.

The result is a collection that women across Pakistan have trusted a wardrobe you can build on, wash after wash, season after season. From Islamabad to Karachi, our clothing is delivered nationwide with Cash on Delivery.
