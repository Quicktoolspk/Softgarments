Cheat Sheet

If you remember nothing else.

Three quick rules that cover 90% of sizing decisions.

Rule 01

Bust decides the size

For kurtis, tops, dresses and co-ord tops — always order based on your bust. It's the hardest measurement to alter.

Between sizes? Go larger
Fitted look? Go smaller
Rule 02

Hip decides bottoms

For trousers, shorts and pyjamas — order based on your hip, not your waist. Waists are easier to adjust at a tailor.

Curvy hips? Size up
Straight hips? True to chart
Rule 03

Re-measure every 6 months

Bodies change — post-pregnancy, weight shifts, fitness routines. What fit you last year might not fit you today.
